### Authentication Specifications ###

## Registration ##
New users are able to register an account with a username, email, and password.

# Username #
The username must be less than 30 characters long, contains only [a-zA-Z0-9], '-', '_' characters. 

# Password #
The entered password must contain at least 8 characters, and may contain the special characters '!', '@', '#', '$', '%', '^', '&', '*'.
